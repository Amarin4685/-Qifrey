About Olruggio

Olruggio, also known as "Master Olly" or the "Torch," is a highly skilled, compassionate, and deeply dedicated witch from the manga and anime series Witch Hat Atelier. Serving as a "Watchful Eye" for the Pointed Cap Witches, he currently resides at Qifrey's Atelier, where he balances his role as a strict yet nurturing mentor to the young apprentices with his passion for inventing practical magic.
