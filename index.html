About Qifrey

Qifrey is a gentle, patient, and highly passionate teacher in the manga series Witch Hat Atelier, known for deeply valuing the individuality of his apprentices. While he presents a light-hearted and kind personality to his students and colleagues, he harbors a dark past and a deeply buried resentment toward the Brimmed Caps (Brimhats), who used his body as a human guinea pig during his childhood and took his right eye.
